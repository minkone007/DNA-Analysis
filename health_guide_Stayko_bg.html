Преглед и главен синтез

Генетичната история - цялостната картина

This guide reads your raw genome with one method for every trait. Every polygenic score is центрирана спрямо тосканска референтна популация (1000 Genomes TSI) and checked against a second Iberian reference (IBS). A z-score of 0 means typical for that reference, and the score is a ranking, not a risk.

Most traits sit close to the average. A tier needs at least 8 scored loci and a score that survives removing its single strongest locus.

  • Една находка за потвърждаване - a single Factor V Leiden copy (rs6025 TC). See the Blood Clots card; it needs a clinical test before anyone relies on it.
  • Повишена: оценка за шизофрения (z +1.97). Умерена: оценка за глаукома (z +1.22).
  • Благоприятни: lupus (z -1.94), prostate cancer (z -2.31), multiple sclerosis (z -1.10), rheumatoid arthritis (z -1.10), alzheimer (z -1.09), ankylosing spondylitis (z -1.20), celiac disease (z -1.01), migraine (z -1.00), colorectal cancer (z -1.07). Celiac and migraine sit at the cut-off, and colorectal is neutral on the second reference.
  • Един белег, който се откроява - the height score is low (z -2.66); your measured height is far more informative.

Не могат да бъдат оценени в този файл: APOE (rs429358 is missing), CYP2C19 *2, VKORC1, key G6PD and HFE C282Y variants, MC1R R151C, DPYD *2A, CYP2D6 copy number, HLA alleles and rare high-risk genes.

Проверка в ClinVar: Ten one- or two-star pathogenic entries matched the file. Nine do not hold up: your genotype does not contain the allele ClinVar lists. The tenth, RBM8A rs201779890 (GG), is also seen in a close relative and its reference allele frequency points to a strand-reporting artefact (unverified), not a true variant. No ClinVar finding survived the check, and the same entries (CAV3 rs1008642 and TG rs2294024) were set aside as annotation artefacts.

Цветът на очите, косата и кожата show genotypes only, without a probability, because the published IrisPlex weights and the official HIrisPlex-S tool were not available.

Проверка на устойчивостта: every tier was rerun on an Iberian (IBS) reference. 9 of 11 tier assignments held. Borderline: Asthma is neutral here and favourable on IBS (z -0.98 against -1.17); Atrial fibrillation is neutral here and favourable on IBS (z -0.91 against -1.13); Colorectal cancer is favourable here and neutral on IBS (z -1.07 against -0.85); Migraine is favourable here and neutral on IBS (z -1.00 against -0.88). All of these are borderline in either reference.

Read this as a set of tendencies, not predictions. Never change a medicine because of this page.

Кръвни съсиреци и фактор V

Оценка и маркери

Факторът V (F5) rs6025 е TC. In catalog rows the T allele is the risk allele for venous thrombosis (three large European studies). Its frequency in the Tuscan reference is close to zero, so one T copy is unusual. The pattern fits being a heterozygous carrier of Factor V Leiden, a common clotting tendency that most carriers never act on. That it is the Leiden allele is from the literature (unverified here).

Приемете това като следа, не като диагноза. It is a single call from a low-pass whole-genome file, and rare-variant calls from this kind of data can be wrong. A clinical Factor V Leiden test is the way to confirm it. If it is confirmed, it is worth telling your doctor before long-haul travel, surgery, or any hormone treatment. Do not change any medicine because of this page.

Prothrombin rs1799963 is GG, so no copy of the other common clotting variant. The venous-thrombosis score (z +1.30, 90th percentile, from 12 loci; without its strongest locus (rs6025) z -0.92) rests almost entirely on rs6025, so it is not ranked as a tier.
