Overview

Julian's Genetic Story - The Big Picture

Every polygenic score is centred on the expected distribution for a child of a Tuscan-type (Italian) father and a Japanese mother, because Julian's ancestry is half Japanese and half Bulgarian. For each variant the expected dosage is the father-side frequency plus the Japanese frequency, and the X chromosome is counted from the Japanese side only. Zero means typical for that parentage; the scores are rankings, not risks. The effect sizes come from European-ancestry studies, so they are less accurate for the Japanese half of his genome. Allele strand was verified at every locus that can be checked (2,036 of 2,036 concordant), and the file is consistent with Minko and Ai as parents (0.3% Mendelian conflicts, in line with genotyping noise; X chromosome from Ai).

This main guide covers traits that are useful or relevant in childhood: growth, blood fats, immune conditions, lifestyle markers and how medicines may be handled. Adult-onset conditions (brain, cancer, heart, eye, longevity and the psychiatric scores) are in a separate parent appendix, kept apart on purpose so that Julian reads only what suits his age and the parents decide when to share the rest.

The strongest signal on this page is psoriasis (higher than expected, robust to the reference), and LDL cholesterol and total cholesterol lean higher, driven partly by an APOE-region stand-in. The type 1 diabetes tier is borderline. Everything else is close to typical.

This is a first draft. The official hair, eye and skin predictor was unreachable, so that card reports genotypes only; the Bulgarian and Japanese versions will follow.
