Your Genetic Story - The Big Picture

Stayko's multi-system genetic profile shares its core architecture with his brother Minko's — both drawing on the same Eastern Balkan ancestral pool — while diverging in specific, meaningful ways across pigmentation, cognition, and disease-risk loci. As with Minko, there is no high-penetrance single-gene disorder in this profile; what's present instead is moderate, polygenic loading distributed across several biological networks, with the specific pattern of loading differing from his brother's in identifiable places:

  • Cardiometabolic system — polygenic tendencies concerning lipid handling and glycemic efficiency, requiring the full cardiovascular and metabolic sections to characterise properly.
  • Pigmentation and physical traits — a broadly similar profile to Minko's at the shared loci (skin tone, hair colour), with real divergence at the hair-loss and secondary eye-colour markers.
  • Cognitive architecture — a COMT genotype (Val/Met, heterozygous) distinct from Minko's Val/Val, placing Stayko at the intermediate point on the classic dopamine-clearance spectrum rather than at Minko's fast-clearance end.

The sections below build out this picture system by system, using the same GWAS Summary / Deeper Analysis structure as Minko's report, with fresh analysis reflecting Stayko's own genotypes throughout.
