Blood Clots & Factor V

Score & Markers

Factor V (F5) rs6025 is TC. In catalog rows the T allele is the risk allele for venous thrombosis (three large European studies). Its frequency in the Tuscan reference is close to zero, so one T copy is unusual. The pattern fits being a heterozygous carrier of Factor V Leiden, a common clotting tendency that most carriers never act on. That it is the Leiden allele is from the literature (unverified here).

Treat this as a lead, not a diagnosis. It is a single call from a low-pass whole-genome file, and rare-variant calls from this kind of data can be wrong. A clinical Factor V Leiden test is the way to confirm it. If it is confirmed, it is worth telling your doctor before long-haul travel, surgery, or any hormone treatment.
