Metabolic Health

mthfr Moderate folate metabolism signal
Moderate folate metabolism signal. You carry both MTHFR C677T (GA heterozygous) and MTHFR A1298C (GT heterozygous) — compound heterozygosity that reduces MTHFR enzyme activity by approximately 50-60%. This impairs conversion of dietary folate to active 5-methylTHF and slows homocysteine clearance. Practical action: supplement with methylfolate (5-MTHF) rather than folic acid, and methylcobalamin rather than cyanocobalamin. Test homocysteine, folate, and B12 blood levels to confirm metabolic status. 5 SNPs combined.
