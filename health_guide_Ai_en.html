This guide reads your raw genome independently of the earlier automated report. Every polygenic score is centred on a Japanese reference population (1000 Genomes JPT), so zero means typical for a Japanese woman, and allele strand was verified at every locus that can be checked (1,405 of 1,405 concordant). No high-penetrance single-gene finding is visible on this file, and most traits sit close to the population average. Where your genome does lean away from average, the pattern is coherent:

  • Metabolic axis - triglyceride-raising variants (APOA5, GCKR), a fatty-liver-leaning combination (PNPLA3, GCKR) and a mild-to-moderate type 2 diabetes loading, mostly through insulin-secretion genes.
  • Favourable atherosclerosis and gout loci - the 9p21 coronary risk haplotype is absent, you lack the ALDH2 flush variant that raises coronary risk in East Asians, and the main Japanese urate variant (ABCG2 Q141K) is absent.

Read this as a set of tendencies, not predictions. Genetic scores capture only part of risk; infection, lifestyle and screening usually matter more.
